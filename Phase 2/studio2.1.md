## Prompt B — Printmaking / Linocut

**Medium:** Linocut printmaking  

**Elements & Principles:** Line, Value, Movement, Texture, Unity  

**Tools:** Carving tools, linoleum block, ink, paper  

**Technique / Process:** Carving, inking, printing, high contrast  

**Composition / Subject:** A quiet street scene at sunset with one person walking under streetlights  

**Visual Prompt:**  
Create a black and white linocut print of a quiet street scene at sunset with one person walking under streetlights. Use bold lines, simple shapes, and strong contrast. The image should look graphic and minimal, with clear edges and a structured composition.

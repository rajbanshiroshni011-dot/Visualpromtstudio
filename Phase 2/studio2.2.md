## Assignment 2.2 — Visual Communication Design + Ways of Seeing

**Chosen Artwork:** Poster for Hydrogen Peroxide Tooth Powder (1927, Shiseido)

**Brand Remix:**  
For this remix, I transformed the original poster into a modern social media style design. I added bold typography like “SHISEIDO™ OFFICIAL” and a “Verified” checkmark to make it look like a trusted brand account. I also added “Trusted since 1927” to connect the past with modern branding. These elements make the artwork look like a current advertisement instead of a historical poster.

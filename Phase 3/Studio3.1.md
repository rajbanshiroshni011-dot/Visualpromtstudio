## Prompt 1

**Historical Theme:** Art connected to survival, ritual, and belief  
**Historical Period:** Paleolithic  
**Artwork Used as Inspiration:** Cave Paintings, Pech Merle  

**Visual Prompt:**  
Create a simple cave painting from the Paleolithic period inspired by Pech Merle. The artwork should be on a rough cave wall using natural colors like black, red, and brown. It should show animals like horses or bison drawn with simple lines and shapes. The texture should look uneven and natural, and the drawing should not look too detailed or realistic. Use repetition and movement to create a sense of rhythm in the composition.

This artwork was made for survival and belief. Early humans depended on animals, so they painted them as part of hunting rituals or spiritual practices. These images were believed to help ensure success in hunting and show a connection between humans, nature, and the spiritual world.

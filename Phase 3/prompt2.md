Historical Theme: Cathedrals as spiritual hubs for travelers and pilgrims
Historical Period: Byzantine
Artwork Used as Inspiration: Hagia Sophia
Visual Prompt:
Create the inside of a large Byzantine cathedral inspired by Hagia Sophia. Show a big dome, tall arches, and columns. Use golden colors and light coming through windows to make the space look bright and spiritual. The building should look very large and balanced, with a calm and peaceful feeling.
This artwork shows the importance of religion in Byzantine society. Cathedrals were built to show power and connect people to God. They were also places where travelers and pilgrims came to worship, so the design was meant to make people feel amazed and close to the divine.
